# Privacy Policy — Trợ lý TikTok

Last updated: 2026-11-22

**What we access.** After you log in with TikTok and approve, the app reads: your display name and open ID (`user.info.basic`); follower, following, like and video counts (`user.info.stats`); and your public videos' titles, descriptions, dates, durations and view/like/comment/share counts (`video.list`).

**Where it is stored.** Only on your own computer (Windows `%APPDATA%\TikTokHelper`). Access tokens are encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app's developer — there is no such server.

**How it is used.** Only to show your channel's statistics and trends to you inside the app. If you choose to use the optional AI analysis, a summary table of these statistics is sent to the AI service (Claude) that you are logged into yourself, to generate content suggestions.

**Sharing.** The data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

**Deletion.** Click "Ngắt kết nối" (Disconnect) in the app and delete the `%APPDATA%\TikTokHelper` folder; you can also revoke access in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).
